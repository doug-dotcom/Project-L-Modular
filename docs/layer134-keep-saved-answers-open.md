# Layer 134 — keep saved answers open on refresh

Refresh review preserves each checked question’s open or closed state by request ID, including entries hidden by filters and older entries loaded again after a refresh. New tasks follow the current Expand all or Collapse all preference. Results and status are fetched again as before.

Expansion choices remain in the active review only. Expand all and Collapse all clear remembered exceptions, including older entries not yet reloaded. Closing the review or changing accounts discards the choices.

Validation covers individual and hidden entries, new tasks, repeated refreshes, older batches, global expansion changes and a newly opened review. Phone visual testing remains outstanding.
